Got questions about Solar Power?

Find clear answers to common questions about solar installations, costs, savings, and subsidies in Bihar.

Installing an on-grid or hybrid solar power system can reduce your monthly electricity bills by 70% to 90%. Excess electricity produced during the day is sent back to the power grid (via net-metering) or stored in batteries for nighttime use.

Yes! Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential rooftop solar installations receive direct bank subsidies: up to ₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, and up to ₹78,000 for 3kW+ systems. Smart Solar Solutions assists you with complete application filing and documentation.

Absolutely. A 3kW to 5kW solar system easily operates 1 to 2 Ton Air Conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, and water pumps. For commercial clients or flour mills (Atta Chakki), we engineer 7.5HP to 50kW+ VFD drive solar plants.

On-Grid: Connected to DISCOM grid without batteries (maximum bill reduction & lowest cost).
Off-Grid: Uses battery bank for areas with frequent power cuts.
Hybrid: Combines grid export with battery backup for uninterrupted power 24/7.
